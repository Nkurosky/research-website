Research Interests

My research interests center on how agents make adaptive decisions in complex environments. I am especially interested in heuristic decision-making, ecological rationality, expertise, strategy learning, and the comparison between human cognition and artificial agents.

My longer-term goal is to study how agents acquire, modify, prune, and reorganize decision strategies across changing environments.

Current Projects

League of Legends Heuristic Decision-Making Study

This project examines how players of different expertise levels use simple cues and heuristics to make decisions in League of Legends scenarios.

CTRNN Capture-the-Flag Agents

This project explores how minimalist neural agents controlled by continuous-time recurrent neural networks can evolve behavior in a capture-the-flag environment.
