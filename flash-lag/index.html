How to use it

Look for the seam.

At the default settings, the moving and flashed segments share one captured angle. Perception can still make the meeting point feel kinked or lagged. Slow the motion, or press Freeze reference, to inspect the geometry.

Display constraint

Nominal timing, not a stopwatch.

Browsers present frames on a display refresh cycle. A 20 ms target usually spans one or more display intervals; the observed visibility depends on refresh rate, workload, and the browser's scheduler.
